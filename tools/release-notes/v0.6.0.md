**The Last of Us Remastered: PC port** 0.6.0

The first Linux package, Intel XeSS and NVIDIA DLSS in the download, anisotropic filtering,
pushed dumpsters and pallets that steer like Part I, a fix for the black screen at startup on AMD +
NVIDIA laptops, smoother level loads on Intel Arc, and a more complete save list in the game's
menu.

> **No game files are included.** You need your own decrypted copy of the game and its update. This
> project is not affiliated with Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog.

**Discord:** help, testing and news: https://discord.gg/9mnjjezcNj

## Fixes and improvements

- **Linux** ([#222](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/pull/222)): the port now runs
  on 64-bit Linux, and this release has its first Linux package, **TLOU-Remastered-linux.zip**.
  It includes its own Python, so open **TLOU-Launcher** after extracting it. It uses your
  distribution's Vulkan driver and libraries (SDL3, FFmpeg, fmt, Zydis, xxHash, X11), and it was
  built on Arch Linux: it needs the same library versions as Arch's (see Known issues). The
  launcher's **Update** button works on Linux too. This package has not been tried on a Linux PC yet; please report how it goes.
- **Intel XeSS** ([#215](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/pull/215)): a new
  anti-aliasing and upscaling choice beside FSR and DLSS, as native anti-aliasing and with the
  quality presets. Intel's XeSS library is now in the package. It needs a GPU whose driver has
  `VK_EXT_mutable_descriptor_type` (Intel Arc, among others) and the Visual C++ 2015-2022
  runtime; elsewhere the game says why and uses FSR 3.1.
- **NVIDIA DLSS** is in the package again: DLAA and the quality presets on RTX cards. On other
  cards the menus say DLSS needs an RTX card and FSR 3.1 runs.
- **Sharper textures when upscaling**: the quality presets of FSR, DLSS and XeSS now sample
  textures at the output size's detail, as these upscalers ask for. Before, textures were as
  blurry as at the lower render size.
- **Anisotropic filtering**: Off (the game's own), 2x, 4x, 8x or 16x for floors, roads and walls
  seen at an angle, in the launcher's **Video** section, **PC SETTINGS** and the overlay menu's
  Image tab. It applies at once.
- **Pushing dumpsters and pallets** ([#211](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/211)):
  with the mouse, the camera now stays free and the object turns towards where you look, as in the
  PC version of Part I. **Old pallet and dumpster steering** in the launcher's **Controls** >
  **Mouse** tab brings back 0.4.0's steering.
- **Scope sensitivity**: a new switch (launcher **Controls** > **Mouse**, the overlay menu and **PC
  SETTINGS**) turns the separate scope sensitivity off, so scopes turn like normal aiming. The
  scope sensitivity's 100% is now what 200% was.
- **Black screen at startup on AMD + NVIDIA laptops**
  ([#194](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/194),
  [#206](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/206)): on laptops with an AMD
  integrated GPU and an NVIDIA one (such as a Ryzen 5 3550H with a GTX 1650), the game stopped at a
  black window. AMD's switchable graphics layer, which hung there, is now turned off for the game.
  Confirmed fixed with a test build.
- **Wall stripes on RTX cards** ([#58](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/58)):
  the ambient occlusion shader read some pixels exactly between two texels, and RTX cards picked
  either neighbor in a repeating pattern: vertical stripes fixed to the screen. It now reads the
  texel's center. This should fix the stripes; please report if you still see them.
- **Volumetric light without steps** ([#55](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/55)):
  a new opt-in **Light beams without steps** in the launcher's **Video** section draws the sun's,
  moon's, spotlights' and flashlights' light beams from camera rays: no steps in the beams, no halo
  around characters and no boxes at openings. It is not the PS4's look and costs a little more GPU
  time. While the game is paused or in photo mode it is no longer slow with this option on (on a
  GTX 1050 Ti, from 4 to about 15 FPS).
- **Flickering line along the bottom and right edges**
  ([#188](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/188)): with FSR 3.1, TAA or XeSS,
  a bright line flickered every other frame along the screen's bottom and right edges, on the
  title menu too. Fixed.
- **Smoother level loads on Intel Arc** ([#216](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/pull/216)):
  on Intel GPUs the game now uses a separate copy queue for data the GPU wrote. On an Arc B580 the
  stalls of 100 ms or more at a level load went from 6-8 to 0-1, and walking from 63 to 66 FPS.
  Other GPUs are unchanged.
- **Switching to TAA in the menu** no longer crashes.
- **Update 1.11 folder in the launcher**: the **Folders** window has an **Update 1.11** row to
  choose the update's folder when it is not beside the game. **Play** warns when the game would
  run without update 1.11 and offers to open **Folders**.
- **The save list in the game's menu**:
  - Each save shows its sub-chapter's picture, Left Behind's saves their own chapter's, and the
    rest of the save's detail (completion, play time, collectibles).
  - Saving over an existing save asks first.
  - A new game's or chapter's save list can **continue without saving** again (Square).
  - The list's pictures no longer stay over the game when Start resumes it, and their memory is
    freed when the list closes.
  - A list with a single save (Left Behind's) shows the save's name.
- **The game's menu buttons and pages no longer disappear in long sessions**: after about 18 menu
  transitions, QUIT TO DESKTOP, PC SETTINGS and the save list were left out until the next start.
- **Saves of dumps without `playgo-chunk.dat`** load again; the launcher says when neither the
  game nor the update folder has that file.
- **Job workers** wait less when idle: on a GTX 1050 Ti, fewer frames over 50 ms on the same route.
- **Crash reports**: a crash inside the port's GPU code now writes its cause to the log instead of
  ending with only "libunwind: pc not in table".
- The launcher's **Debug** tab (**Game menu**) can turn off the game menu additions (QUIT, PC
  SETTINGS, the save list), to compare if something looks wrong.

## Update

**Windows, from 0.1.8 or newer:** use the launcher's **Update** button. **From 0.1.7 or older:**
extract the zip over your previous folder. Your saves, settings and logs are in its `analysis`
folder.

**Linux:** this is the first Linux package: follow New install. From a source checkout, keep its
`analysis` folder (copy it into the extracted package to keep your saves and settings).

## New install

**Windows**

1. Download **TLOU-Remastered-windows.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
2. Open **TLOU Launcher.exe**.
3. Click **Folders** and choose **Game files**: the folder with `eboot.bin`. Update 1.11 is looked
   for beside it, in the folder of the same name ending in `-patch`, or choose it in **Update 1.11**.
4. Click **Play**. You can also open **TLOU Game.exe** to start with your saved launcher settings.

Intel integrated graphics (6th generation and newer) need Intel's own current driver for Vulkan
1.3; a laptop maker's older driver can stop at Vulkan 1.2.

**Linux**

1. Install your distribution's Vulkan driver (Mesa or NVIDIA) and the libraries the game uses
   (on Arch: `sudo pacman -S --needed vulkan-icd-loader sdl3 ffmpeg fmt zydis xxhash libx11`).
   `licenses/THIRD-PARTY.txt` in the package lists every library it needs.
2. Download **TLOU-Remastered-linux.zip** below and extract it to a folder with plenty of free space.
3. Open **TLOU-Launcher** (from a terminal: `./TLOU-Launcher`), then follow steps 3 and 4 above.
   **TLOU-Game** starts with your saved launcher settings.

## Known issues

- The Linux package was built on Arch Linux (November 2026) and needs the same major versions of
  its libraries: FFmpeg 9 (`libavcodec.so.63`), fmt 12, Zydis 4.1 and SDL3. On a distribution
  with older ones (Ubuntu, Debian, Fedora, ...) it cannot start: build the port from source
  there (README, Building > Linux). It has not been tried on a Linux PC yet.
- Intel integrated graphics are slow (about 10 FPS at 1080p on a UHD 630).
- Volumetric light can look a little odd right at the edges of objects in front of it
  ([#55](https://github.com/ravencaio/tlou-remastered-pc/issues/55)); **Light beams without
  steps** avoids it.
- A thin light ring can remain where wheels and feet touch the ground: the game's own design,
  made thinner by **Contact shadows at half resolution**.
- Short pauses can occur the first time an area is played while its shaders compile.
- FSR 4 is not in this package.
- Dubs from other regions have not been tried with files from a European or Japanese copy yet.
- Online play (Factions) is not available in this port, but it can be played through other forks:
  visit the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/9mnjjezcNj) to learn more.

When reporting a problem, attach `analysis\tlou\boot.log` (**Open log** in the launcher), or ask
on the Discord server.
